# Evlek

**Find the right home with Evlek.**

Explore homes, land and commercial property for sale and rent across North Cyprus, and find the place that fits your life.

Evlek (evlek.app) is a Northern Cyprus property portal for homes, commercial units and land for sale and long-term rent. Only agents and agencies approved by Evlek publish listings. The name comes from an old Cypriot land measure: 1 evlek = 334.45 m².
